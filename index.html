Case Study - Product design

Redesigning the Aer Lingus booking flow

I’ve worked as a Product Designer at Aer Lingus since 2016. In that time I’ve delivered a broad range of large projects including full redesigns for rebrand, accessibility compliance and responsive web projects, as well as ongoing optimisation and BAU work.

I redesigned all 7 steps of the booking flow for the Aer Lingus website which takes over 3 million bookings annually and generates over 65% of business revenue.

Case study - Designing for web accessibility

Complying with U.S. accessibility legislation

U.S. accessibility legislation pre-dates the E.U. equivalent by almost 20 years. As an airline that travels to and from the United States, Aer Lingus has had to comply with U.S. Department of Transport accessibility standards since 2017.

I’ve worked extensively on bringing design standards into compliance with W3C/WCAG accessibility standards since 2017.

Case study - Brand refresh, redesign and development

Rebuilding Bohemian FC’s web presence

Founded in 1890, Bohemian FC are the oldest football club in continuous existence in Ireland. During the financial crisis, Bohemians almost went out of business and needed to rely on the goodwill of volunteers, supporters and community members to survive.

I completely rebuilt The Bohemian FC web presence on a voluntary basis, helping the club to now boast the highest commercial/merchandise income in the League of Ireland.
